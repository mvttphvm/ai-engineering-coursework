# Service status, outages, and incident updates

When something is broken on our side, here is where to check, what the status
terms mean, and how to get notified so you do not have to keep refreshing.

## Check current status

The live status page at **status.resolve.example.com** shows the current state
of the API, the web app, the agent service, and email delivery, plus a history
of incidents for the last 90 days. If your problem is listed there, it is a
known issue on our side and you do not need to open a ticket.

## Status terms

- **Operational** — the service is working normally.
- **Degraded performance** — the service works but is slower than usual, or a
  minor feature is unreliable.
- **Partial outage** — one component (for example, the agent service) is down
  while others work.
- **Major outage** — a core service such as sign-in or the API is down for most
  users.

## Get notified

On the status page, click **Subscribe to updates** to get incident emails, or
follow the RSS feed if you prefer. During a major outage we post updates at
least every 60 minutes, and a post-incident report (what happened, what we are
changing) within 5 business days.

## Our uptime commitment

Paid plans include a 99.9% monthly uptime commitment for the API and web app.
If we miss it, account owners can request a service credit of 10% of the
monthly fee for each full hour below the commitment, up to 100% of the monthly
fee. Request credits within 30 days of the incident under
**Settings → Billing → Request service credit**. Scheduled maintenance is
announced at least 72 hours ahead and does not count against uptime.
