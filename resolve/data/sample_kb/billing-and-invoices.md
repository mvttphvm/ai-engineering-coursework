# Billing, payment methods, and invoices

This article explains how billing works, how to update your payment method,
and where to find invoices for your records.

## Billing cycle

Paid plans are billed monthly or annually, depending on the option you chose at
checkout. Your billing date is the day of the month you first subscribed. Annual
plans are charged once per year on the anniversary of your start date. All
charges appear on your statement as **RESOLVE\*SUBSCRIPTION**.

## Update your payment method

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Payment method**.
2. Click **Replace card** and enter the new card details.
3. Click **Save**. The new card becomes the default for the next charge.

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. We do not accept
prepaid cards for annual plans. Updating your card does not change your billing
date and does not trigger an immediate charge, unless you have an overdue
balance, which will be charged to the new card within 24 hours.

## Failed payments and grace period

If a payment fails, we retry the card after 1, 3, and 7 days. Your account
enters a 14-day grace period during which all features keep working. If the
payment still fails after 14 days, the account is downgraded to the Free plan
and paid features are paused. Your data is never deleted for non-payment.

## Download invoices and receipts

Go to **Settings → Billing → Invoice history**. Every charge has a downloadable
PDF invoice and receipt. To add a company name, VAT ID, or a billing address to
future invoices, edit **Settings → Billing → Invoice details**; changes apply
to invoices issued after the edit and cannot be applied retroactively.

## Taxes

Prices are shown exclusive of tax. Where required, VAT or sales tax is added at
checkout based on your billing address and shown as a separate line item on the
invoice.
