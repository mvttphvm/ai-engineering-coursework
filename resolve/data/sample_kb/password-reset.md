# How to reset your password

If you forgot your password or want to change it for security reasons, you can
reset it in a few minutes. You do not need to contact support for a password
reset unless your account is locked.

## Reset from the sign-in page

1. Go to the sign-in page and click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter the email address associated with your account and click **Send reset
   link**.
3. Open the email titled "Reset your password" and click the link inside. The
   link expires after 60 minutes.
4. Choose a new password. Passwords must be at least 12 characters and include
   one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one symbol.
5. Click **Save**. You will be signed out of all other devices automatically.

## Reset while signed in

Go to **Settings → Security → Change password**. Enter your current password,
then your new password twice, and click **Update password**.

## Didn't receive the reset email?

- Check your spam and promotions folders.
- Wait up to 10 minutes; reset emails can be delayed.
- Make sure you entered the exact email address on your account. If you signed
  up with Google or Apple sign-in, you must reset the password with that
  provider instead.
- If you requested several reset links, only the newest link works.

## Account locked after too many attempts

After 5 failed sign-in attempts your account locks for 30 minutes. You can
either wait 30 minutes and try again, or complete a password reset, which
unlocks the account immediately.

## Still stuck?

Contact support with the email address on the account and the approximate time
you requested the reset. Support can verify whether a reset email was sent, but
support agents can never see or set your password for you.
