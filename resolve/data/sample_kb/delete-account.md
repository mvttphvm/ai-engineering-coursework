# Delete your account and your data

Deleting your account is permanent. This article explains what gets deleted,
what we must keep by law, how long deletion takes, and how to export your data
first.

## Before you delete

- Export your data: go to **Settings → Privacy → Export my data**. We email you
  a download link containing your profile, workspaces, and documents. The export
  can take up to 24 hours for large accounts.
- Cancel any paid subscription first. Deleting an account does not automatically
  refund unused time; see the refund policy for eligibility.
- If you are the owner of a team workspace, you must transfer ownership to
  another admin or delete the workspace before deleting your personal account.

## How to delete your account

1. Go to **Settings → Privacy → Delete account**.
2. Read the summary of what will be deleted and type **DELETE** to confirm.
3. Enter your password (or confirm with your sign-in provider).
4. Click **Permanently delete my account**.

You receive a confirmation email immediately. There is a 14-day grace period
during which you can cancel the deletion by signing in and clicking **Cancel
deletion** on the banner. After 14 days the deletion runs and cannot be undone.

## What gets deleted and what we keep

Deleted permanently: your profile, documents, workspaces you own, support
history, and uploaded files. Backups containing your data are purged within 90
days.

Kept by legal requirement: invoices and payment records (7 years, for tax and
accounting law) and records of abuse reports (2 years). These records are
disconnected from your profile, are never used for marketing, and are listed in
the deletion confirmation email.

## Deleting data for someone else

We can only process deletion requests from the verified account holder. If you
need data removed from an account you cannot sign in to (for example, a deceased
family member's account), contact support with the account email and
documentation; requests of this kind take up to 30 days to review.
