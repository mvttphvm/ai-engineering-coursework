# Shipping and delivery for hardware orders

Resolve sells a small range of physical hardware (desk phones and conference
speaker kits) through the online store. This article covers processing times,
delivery options, tracking, and what to do when an order goes wrong.

## Processing and delivery times

Orders placed before 2:00 PM Pacific Time ship the same business day; orders
after that ship the next business day. We do not ship on weekends or US
holidays.

- Standard shipping: 3–5 business days, free for orders over $75, otherwise
  $6.95.
- Express shipping: 1–2 business days, $19.95.
- We ship within the United States and Canada only. Canadian orders may be
  charged duties and taxes on delivery.

## Tracking your order

When your order ships you receive an email with a tracking number and a link to
the carrier's tracking page. Tracking information can take up to 24 hours to
appear after the label is created. You can also find tracking under
**Store → Orders → Order details**.

## Change or cancel an order

You can change the shipping address or cancel the order from **Store → Orders**
any time before it ships. Once an order has shipped, the address cannot be
changed. If you need a shipped order stopped, contact support; we can request a
carrier intercept for a $12 fee, but intercepts are not guaranteed.

## Damaged, wrong, or missing items

- Damaged or wrong item: contact support within 30 days with a photo of the
  item and packaging. We ship a free replacement and email you a prepaid return
  label.
- Package marked delivered but not received: first check with neighbors and
  your building's mail room, and wait one full business day — carriers sometimes
  mark packages early. If it is still missing after that, contact support and
  we will open a carrier investigation, which takes 5 to 8 business days.

## Returns of hardware

Unopened hardware can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Opened
hardware can be returned within 30 days with a $15 restocking fee. Start a
return under **Store → Orders → Return**. Refunds for returned hardware are
issued after the item arrives at our warehouse, usually within 5 business days.
