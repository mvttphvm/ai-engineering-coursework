# Plans, upgrades, and downgrades

Resolve offers three plans: Free, Pro, and Team. This article covers what each
plan includes and what happens when you change plans mid-cycle.

## Plan comparison

- **Free**: 1 user, 50 documents, community support, 7-day data history.
- **Pro** ($12 per user/month billed annually, $15 billed monthly): unlimited
  documents, 3 agent runs per day, priority email support, 1-year data history.
- **Team** ($25 per user/month billed annually, $30 billed monthly): everything
  in Pro, unlimited agent runs, shared workspaces, SSO sign-in, audit log,
  dedicated support with a 4-hour response target during business hours.

## Upgrade

Upgrades take effect immediately. Go to **Settings → Billing → Change plan**,
choose the new plan, and confirm. You are charged a prorated amount for the
remainder of the current billing period — for example, upgrading halfway
through a month charges roughly half the price difference. Features of the new
plan are available as soon as the payment succeeds.

## Downgrade

Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period, so you keep
your paid features until then and are not charged the lower price early. If
your usage exceeds the lower plan's limits when the downgrade lands (for
example, more documents than Free allows), your content becomes read-only —
nothing is deleted — until you remove content or upgrade again.

## Add or remove seats (Team plan)

Team billing is per seat. Adding a seat charges a prorated amount immediately.
Removing a seat takes effect at the next renewal and does not generate a refund
for the current period. Users removed from a workspace lose access immediately,
but their documents stay in the workspace.

## Cancel your subscription

Cancel under **Settings → Billing → Cancel subscription**. Cancellation is a
downgrade to Free at the end of the billing period; your data is kept and your
documents remain accessible read-only above the Free limits. You can resubscribe
at any time and full access is restored.
