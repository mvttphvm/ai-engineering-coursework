# Refund policy

We want you to be confident in your purchase. This article explains when
refunds are available, how to request one, and how long they take.

## Monthly plans

Monthly subscriptions can be refunded in full within 14 days of the initial
purchase or renewal, as long as the account has not exceeded fair-use limits
(more than 1,000 API calls or 100 agent runs in the period). Refunds requested
after 14 days are not available for monthly plans, but you can cancel at any
time and keep access until the end of the paid month.

## Annual plans

Annual subscriptions can be refunded within 30 days of purchase or renewal.
After 30 days, annual refunds are prorated only if our service was unavailable
for more than 24 consecutive hours due to an outage on our side.

## How to request a refund

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Request a refund**, or contact support.
2. Tell us the invoice number and the reason for the request.
3. We review requests within 2 business days and email you the decision.

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method only. We cannot
refund to a different card, PayPal, or bank account. Refunds take 5 to 10
business days to appear on your statement, depending on your bank.

## What is not refundable

- Add-on purchases (extra seats, extra storage, one-time credits) after they
  have been used.
- Charges from app-store subscriptions (Apple App Store or Google Play) —
  these must be refunded by the store where you purchased.
- Accounts terminated for violating the Terms of Service.

## Chargebacks

If you dispute a charge with your bank instead of contacting us, your account
is paused while the dispute is reviewed, which usually takes 30 to 45 days.
Contacting support first is almost always faster.
